A lightning strike or lightning bolt is a lightning event in which an electric discharge takes place between the atmosphere and the ground. Lightning strikes the average commercial aircraft at least once a year, but modern engineering and design means this is rarely a problem. The movement of aircraft through clouds can even cause lightning strikes. Here we discuss the available detection techniques for, and observations of, planetary lightning by spacecraft, planetary landers and, increasingly, sophisticated terrestrial radio telescopes. We present a comprehensive theory on the formation of ball lightning, a luminous sphere sometimes observed after normal lightning.
