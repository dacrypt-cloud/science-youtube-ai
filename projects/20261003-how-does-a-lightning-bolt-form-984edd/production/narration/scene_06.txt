Lightning involves a near-instantaneous release of energy on a scale averaging between 200 megajoules and 7 gigajoules. We have used machine learning techniques to successfully hindcast nearby and distant lightning hazards by looking at single-site observations of meteorological parameters. Most NBEs are quite peculiar in the sense that very frequently they occur in isolation from other lightning processes. The results encourage further analysis on how mining techniques could contribute to further our understanding of lightning dependencies on atmospheric parameters. Based on the reports for 1023 fatalities associated with natural hazard processes in Switzerland during the period from 1946 to 2015, more than 16% of the cases were caused by lightning, making it the second most frequent cause of loss of life among the natural hazards in Switzerland.
