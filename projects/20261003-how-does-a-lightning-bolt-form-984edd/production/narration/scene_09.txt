Lightning influences the global atmospheric electrical circuit and atmospheric chemistry and is a natural ignition source of wildfires. Lightning was considered to be the second cause of death making natural disasters after flashfloods but preventive measures have slithered it down to the third place in developed countries. A key challenge is in the interpretation of electrical or magnetic signatures claimed to be whistler-mode emissions from lightning discharges. Lightning experts had hardly developed any concurrence on lightning physics, and the observation of dark lightning complicated the Gordian knot. However, lightning itself is viewed as an extraterrestrial energy source. The negative sprite‐parent lightning took place in two thunderstorms that were associated with a tropical disturbance in east central and south Florida.
